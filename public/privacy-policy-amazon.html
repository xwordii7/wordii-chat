Wordii Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Wordii Chat is a messaging and social communication application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Wordii Chat may process account information such as email addresses, usernames, user IDs, profile details, messages, photos, videos, status updates, and other content you choose to share.

Authentication information is processed through Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider rather than displayed to other users.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide accounts, messaging, profiles, media sharing, application functionality, security, and support.

3. Third-Party Services

Wordii Chat integrates Google Firebase services for authentication, database functionality, and file storage. The application also includes Google AdMob integration for advertising. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information according to their applicable policies and user choices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Messages and Shared Content

Messages and shared content may be stored and processed to provide communication features. Content shared with other users may be accessible to its intended recipients or audience. Message display transformations should not be assumed to provide end-to-end encryption.

5. Permissions

Some features may request access to your device camera, microphone, photos, or files. These permissions are used for the relevant features when you choose to use them, subject to your device settings.

6. Data Security

We use available platform and service security features to help protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained as needed to operate the service, maintain security, and meet applicable requirements. To request account or personal data deletion, contact us at officialwordii@gmail.com. We will review and respond to deletion requests, subject to applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Users should comply with the applicable minimum age requirements and any parental consent requirements. We may take appropriate action when notified of an account that does not meet applicable requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions and data requests, email: officialwordii@gmail.com
