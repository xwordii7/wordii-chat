Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Wordii Chat account or selected personal data.

1. Delete My Account

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data, including profile information and eligible stored content.

Request Account Deletion

2. Delete My Data Only

Keep your account active while requesting deletion of selected data, such as your profile photo, status updates, or other eligible personal content.

Request Data Deletion

Include your Wordii Chat username, the type of deletion requested, and the data you want deleted where applicable.

We will verify account ownership before processing a request. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary for security.

Do not send passwords or OTP codes.

Contact: officialwordii@gmail.com
